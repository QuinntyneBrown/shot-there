## Best practices

### Layout

- Place the badge right after what it qualifies — the fee amount, the permit row, the studio name — so it reads in place.
- Long words wrap inside the pill rather than overflowing.

### Content

- Sentence case, two to five words, no full stop: "Mandatory", "Not required", "Lowest", "Due soon".
- Visitor warnings say what may be wrong: "Prices may be out of date". Owner flags are terse: "Prices stale".
- Never use a badge for a record's state ("Draft") — that is `st-status` — or for a fact about the place ("Cyc wall").

### Accessibility

- The words carry the meaning; tone colour and the dot only support them (WCAG 1.4.1).
- Badges are never focusable; a related action is its own button or link.
