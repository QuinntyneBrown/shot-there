The DS badge: a short word in a pill that qualifies what sits beside it — "$25.00 Mandatory", "$91.00 Lowest". The `st-badge` host is the `.badge` pill, with `.badge--info`, `.badge--success`, `.badge--warning` or `.badge--danger` for its `tone`, and `.badge--solid` for the filled look.

The default slot is the word; a dot drawn in CSS precedes it. Badges are plain text with no role and are never interactive.
