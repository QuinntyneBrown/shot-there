The DS icon button: a 44px round control holding one outline glyph and no visible text. `st-icon-button` is an attribute on a native `<button>` or `<a>`, so `type`, `disabled` and `href` stay the consumer's. The host carries `.icon-btn`, plus `.icon-btn--outline` with `outline`.

`label` becomes the host's `aria-label`; the glyph inside is an `st-icon` and is hidden. ShotThere uses it only over a photo and on a map: the lightbox's Close, Previous photo and Next photo, and the map popup's Close.
