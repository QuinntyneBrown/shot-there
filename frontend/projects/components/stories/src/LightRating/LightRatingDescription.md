The DS light rating (`.light-rating`, on the host): `st-light-rating` shows three pips filled by `level` — none 0, low 1, medium 2, strong 3 — followed by `text`, the word that is both the visible and the accessible value.

It rates the light at one time of day: in a studio's light tiles or in a spot's light table, beside the time of day that names it.
