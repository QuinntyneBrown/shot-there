## Best practices

### Layout

- Public pages only: no footer in the admin, on the owner sign-in or on the map.
- The application projects its email signup into `slot="signup"`, under the tagline.

### Content

- Tagline, word for word: "Toronto photo studios and outdoor spots, reviewed first-hand. Nothing is listed unless it has been shot there."
- Links in order: Studios, Spots, Guides, Editorial policy, YouTube channel. No social icons, partner logos or "Advertise with us".
- The application builds the copy line and its year: "© 2026 ShotThere · No paid placement: order comes only from the sort you choose."

### Accessibility

- The brand is text, not a heading and not a link.
- `navLabel` names the landmark ("Footer"); the external link's name ends with "(opens in a new tab)".
