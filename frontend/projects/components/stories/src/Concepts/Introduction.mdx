## Principles

- **The evidence does the talking.** A white canvas, near-black ink and photos at their real proportions; prices,
  scores and measurements carry the page.
- **One hue, one meaning.** The warm red accent means "selected / you are here" — the active filter, the current tab,
  focus. Info is quiet stone, not blue.
- **One primary button per screen.** The solid ink action; everything else is secondary, ghost or link.
- **State is ARIA.** Pressed, busy, invalid and current are `aria-pressed`, `aria-busy`, `aria-invalid` and
  `aria-current` — never a class alone.
- **Phone first.** Every screen works at phone width without horizontal scrolling, with 44px targets.
