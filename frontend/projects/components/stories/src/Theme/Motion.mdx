# Motion

Motion is brief and useful: `fast` for hover and press, `base` for fades and toasts, `slow` for dialogs and sheets.
Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` every transition duration drops to 0.01ms, so components need no media queries
of their own. The loop durations stay: components stop decorative loops (shimmer, pulse) themselves, and the spinner
keeps turning because it reports status.

Hover a row to watch the dot travel.
