The DS link on a native `<a>`. `a[st-link]` is drawn by the foundations' link rule: ink, underlined, accent on hover. `standalone` adds `.link--standalone` (a 44px label-weight link on its own line); `external` adds `.link--external`, `target="_blank"`, `rel="noopener"`, the trailing external arrow and a visually hidden `externalHint` ("(opens in a new tab)").

`href` and `routerLink` stay the consumer's; the default slot is the accessible name.
