The DS stat grid (`.stat-grid`, on the host): `ul[st-stat-grid]` is the list that opens the administration dashboard. It lays its stat tiles out in one column, then three equal columns from 48rem, separated by `--layout-gutter`.

Put `li[st-stat-tile]` items in it, or three `li[st-stat-tile-skeleton]` items while the dashboard loads.
