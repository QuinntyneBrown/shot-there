The DS disclosure: the quiet, fixed callout at the end of a studio's "Verdict" or a spot's "Best uses" that says how the session behind the review was paid for, with a link to the editorial policy. `st-disclosure` renders an info icon, a title paragraph from `title`, then the statement and link projected as its content.

The host carries the `.disclosure` class (title `.disclosure__title`): a sunken block with a strong left border. It looks the same whatever the answer, so no review reads as more or less trustworthy from its styling.
