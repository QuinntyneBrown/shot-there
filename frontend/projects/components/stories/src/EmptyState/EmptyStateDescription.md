The DS empty state: what shows where a list or section has nothing yet, nothing matching, or could not load. The `st-empty-state` host is the `.empty` block — an optional round `icon`, a `title` heading (`h2` by default, or `headingLevel` 3 or 4), optional `text`, and the actions.

`tone` adds `.empty--info`, `.empty--success` or `.empty--error`, which tint the icon; `compact` adds `.empty--compact` for tighter padding. Actions are links or buttons with `slot="actions"`.
