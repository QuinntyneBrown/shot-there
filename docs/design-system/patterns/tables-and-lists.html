Pattern

Data tables and lists

ShotThere is a comparison site, so its lists and tables carry the same facts in the same order everywhere: all-in cost, score, review or verification date. Every order is one the visitor or the admin chose, never a paid one, and every list stays readable at 320px.

Overview

  • Location card grid (.location-grid): studio and spot lists, home's Latest reviews, guides index. Name, neighbourhood and date, then the two facts that compare: all-in for 2 hours and score (studios) or best uses and permit summary (spots).
  • Comparison table (.compare-table): 2 to 4 studios as columns, grouped rows, a fixed row-label column.
  • Measured-spec tables: description lists (.kv) for one-value facts (Usable shooting space, Ceiling height), and tables with .table--stack for facts by row (light by time of day and season, permits by shoot type) that become label-over-value cards below 48rem.
  • Admin lists: .table.table--stack for the studio, spot and guide lists (name, status, last updated, flags, Edit), and .admin-list rows for the dashboard's re-verification list.
  • Guide lists: .guide-index of guides, the ranked .guide-list of entries, and .image-row--entry rows in the guide editor.
  • List rows (.list): short related lists such as Rain backup.

Responsive behaviour

  • Location grid: one column at XS, two from SM, three from LG; beside the filter sidebar two at LG and three at XL.
  • Comparison: below LG the studio columns scroll inside .table-wrap while the row-label column stays fixed; each studio column is at least 160px and the page itself never scrolls sideways. From LG up to four studios fit with no scroll.
  • .table--stack below 48rem: the header row is hidden visually, each row becomes a bordered card with its row header as the title and each cell's data-label above its value; Edit shows without hover.
  • Guide entries put the card beside the body from 48rem and stack it above below.
  • Admin rows wrap their action under the text on narrow phones.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native tables with a <caption> (visually hidden when the heading already says it), scope="col", scope="row" and scope="rowgroup"; the admin list's sorted column carries aria-sort="descending". Lists are <ul>, or <ol> where order is meaning (guide entries, entry rows). Card facts are <dl>.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabOne stop per card (the name), then its Compare toggle; one Edit per admin row.
Shift+scroll or ← → in the tableScrolls the comparison's studio columns below LG; the scroll area is focusable.
Space / Enter on CompareToggles the studio in the comparison.

Focus

The whole card shows the focus ring when its name link has focus. Sorting or filtering never moves focus; the new count is announced instead.

Labelling

Repeated actions carry the item in hidden text ("Compare Kensington Window Room", "Edit Ossington Blackbox"). Scores read "3.2 out of 5"; "/ 5" is hidden from assistive technology. Lowest, stale and due-soon flags are words, never colour alone. List result counts are polite live regions: "7 studios", "5 studios, sorted by all-in cost".

Touch

The whole card is the target for its review; Compare and Edit are 44px on phones; stacked rows sit 12px apart.

Content

  • Money in CAD with cents: "$80.00", "$145.00". All-in is always "for 2 hours" (or "All-in 2 h" in a tight card).
  • Measurements metric first, imperial in brackets: "6.1 m × 4.3 m (20 ft × 14 ft)"; in the comparison, metric only to keep columns narrow.
  • Dates "3 Oct 2026" in lists and tables; "Reviewed 3 Oct 2026"; age in brackets where it is the point: "(225 days ago)".
  • Missing values are "—" in tables, and words where the review states them: "No", "None", "Nothing costs extra".
  • Flags: "Prices may be out of date", "Permit rules may be out of date" (public), "Prices stale", "Permits stale", "Due soon", "Stale" (admin); "Lowest" in the comparison.
