Pattern

Notifications

ShotThere tells people things at three distances: a field message beside the control, an alert in the section it concerns, and a toast for the result of an action. The closer the message is to its cause, the longer it stays, and nothing that needs an action ever disappears on its own.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.toast.toast-region.alert.field__error.field__warning

Overview

  • Inline field messages (inline message): .field__error under a control ("Enter an amount of 0 or more"), .field__warning for advice that doesn't block ("You changed a price. Confirm the date you verified it.").
  • Alerts (.alert): a message that belongs to a section or a form and stays while its cause does: "Prices may be out of date", "Permit rules may be out of date", the sign-in answers, the comparison link notes, a failure inside a dialog, "Studio created as a draft".
  • Toasts (.toast in .toast-region): the result of an action whose own control can't show it. Two sets: public toasts (Link copied, Couldn't copy the link, You can compare up to 4 studios) and admin toasts (Saved with a new address, … is published, … is unpublished, Couldn't save …).
  • Flags (badge): passive, always-on notices on cards, the summary panel and admin rows: "Prices may be out of date", "Prices stale", "Due soon".

ShotThere sends no in-app notifications and shows no notification count: there are no visitor accounts. The only email is the new-review email visitors sign up for.

Responsive behaviour

  • Toasts span the screen with a 16px gutter on phones and clear the bottom safe area; from 36rem they are 24rem wide, bottom right. Where the compare bar is shown the region is raised above it.
  • Dismiss buttons on toasts and alerts grow to 44px on phones.
  • Alerts fill their section's width and wrap; titles and file names break rather than widen the page.
  • Stale flags on cards and in the comparison wrap inside narrow columns (white-space: normal on the badge in the table).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Toasts follow the APG Alert pattern for danger and a polite status region for the rest; alerts are static content with role="status" or role="alert" by tone of consequence, not colour. A live region must exist before content is put in it, so the toast region is in the page from the start and empty.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches a toast's action and Dismiss after the current focus point; toasts never take focus themselves.
Enter on Try again or View public pageRuns the action; the toast closes when it succeeds.
Enter on DismissCloses the toast or alert; focus moves to the next toast or back to the page.

Focus

No notification moves focus, with one exception: the form summary. A refused Compare keeps focus on the button that was pressed.

Labelling

Each tone has its own icon shape (circle check, triangle, circled bang, circled i) and its title says the fact in words, so meaning never rests on colour. Dismiss is named "Dismiss". Toast actions are real links or buttons with visible text.

Motion

Toasts slide up 16px and fade out; reduced motion removes the movement. The progress line is decorative and stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Dismiss and toast actions are at least 44px on phones; toasts never cover the compare bar's buttons.

Content

  • Toast titles state the result with the item's name: "Corktown Garage Studio is published", "The Junction Loft is unpublished", "Couldn't save Corktown Garage Studio", "Link copied". The text adds what happens next: "It appears in lists, the map and the sitemap within a minute."
  • Warnings say what might be wrong and what to do: "We last checked these prices 225 days ago. Confirm with the studio before you book."
  • Failures say what was kept: "Your changes are still here." "Nothing changed."
  • No Undo on admin toasts: Unpublish is the reverse of Publish and asks first.
  • Titles carry no full stop ("You've signed out", "Too many tries"); the text under them is full sentences with stops.

Do and don't

Sources

  • Public toasts: success, warning, danger
  • Admin toasts: info, success, warning, danger, stacked
  • Alerts: studio/stale, spot/stale-permit, compare/unknown-slug, sign-in/incorrect, sign-in/session-expired, studio-editor/success, unpublish/failed, admin-dashboard/partial
  • Field messages: studio-editor/invalid, price-changed, upload-failed

Drift fixed during extraction: danger toasts carry role="alert" inside a role="status" region, so some screen readers announce them twice or politely; the system keeps the polite region for info, success and warning and renders danger toasts into a separate assertive container (CSS need listed). The rate-limited warnings use role="alert" while the stale warnings use role="status"; both are right (a blocked action versus a standing fact) and are now documented as the rule. Alert titles mix full stops ("You've signed out.", "We couldn't subscribe you just now.") and none ("Your session expired"); the system drops the stop from every alert and toast title. The compare-limit toast is raised by a fixed --space-24, but at 360px the compare bar wraps to about 122px, so the toast overlaps the bar by 26px; the example raises it clear of the wrapped bar and a CSS rule is listed as a need. The created-studio alert's Dismiss uses the full-size icon while toasts use the small one; both are kept, matching their surfaces.
