Form layout

ShotThere has two kinds of form: the long studio, spot and guide editors the owner fills in after a shoot, and the short owner sign-in. Both use the same parts: field rows, fieldsets, an error summary that links to each problem, and actions that say what happened.

Overview

Form layout arranges form fields; it does not style them. Every form is a native <form novalidate>: the browser's own bubbles are off and the server's messages are shown instead, because the server is the authority for every rule.

  • Editor forms (.editor-form): the studio, spot and guide editors. A column of collapsible sections (Editor layout) beside the status panel, ending in a sticky action bar. One column at XS (L2-054.6).
  • Field rows (.field-row): two short fields that belong together side by side from 36rem, such as Neighbourhood and Address or cross streets, Hourly rate and Minimum booking, Usable width and depth.
  • Fieldsets: a <fieldset> and <legend> around anything answered together: Fees, Natural light, How was this session paid for?, each image group.
  • Form actions (.form-actions.form-actions--sticky): Save changes (or Create studio), Cancel, and the .save-status line, pinned to the bottom of the screen while the form scrolls.
  • Error summary (.form-summary): when a save is refused, a box at the top of the form that counts the problems and links to each field.
  • Sign-in (main.auth): one narrow column with the heading, any alert, then email, password and a full-width Sign in button.

Public pages have no forms except the email signup and the filters. Anything the owner edits opens its own editor screen or a dialog; there are no inline edit forms in lists.

Anatomy

  1. Editor sectionA details.editor-section with an h2 in its summary (here an h3, under this page's own headings); sections sit --space-4 apart in .editor-form.
  2. Field row.field-row: a grid of one column, two from 36rem, --space-4 gap. Only for short fields that are read together.
  3. Fieldset and legendNo border; the legend is --text-label, the group's question or name. A whole optional group says so in the legend with .optional.
  4. Form actions.form-actions--sticky: primary first, then ghost Cancel, on --color-bg-canvas with a hairline top edge and the safe-area inset below. Shown unstuck in this figure.
  5. Save status.save-status, a role="status" line after the buttons: "Unsaved changes", "Saving…", "Saved 13 Oct 2026, 10:02 am".

Field rows

The default row is one column at XS and two from 36rem. Three modifiers cover the other layouts the mocks need: .field-row--pair stays side by side even on phones (minimum and maximum all-in cost in the studio filters), and .field-row--fit (9rem columns, natural light by time of day) and .field-row--fit-sm (7rem columns, the six rubric scores) fit as many tiny fields as the width allows. The mocks still set these columns inline.

Responsive behaviour

  • XS (below 36rem): one column everywhere; every field row stacks except the always-two pair (L2-054.6). The editor's status panel comes first, above the form.
  • SM and up (36rem): field rows go two-up.
  • LG and up (62rem): the status panel moves beside the form and sticks; the form keeps one column of sections (Editor layout).
  • The sticky action bar wraps: Save changes and Cancel stay on one line, the save status drops below them on narrow phones. Its bottom padding adds env(safe-area-inset-bottom), so the buttons clear the home indicator (L2-065).
  • Sign-in: full width with the page margin at XS (fields and button full width, L2-049.4), --space-8 from the header; from 48rem, a 28rem column --space-16 below the header.
  • Nothing scrolls sideways at 320px: grids use minmax(0, 1fr) and labels wrap (L2-064.1).

Theming

Form layout reads semantic tokens only. The summary is --color-danger-fg on --color-danger-bg, with its links in the same colour (color: inherit). The sticky bar is --color-bg-canvas with a --color-border-default hairline above. The save status is --color-fg-muted, --color-success-fg when saved and --color-danger-fg when refused. In forced colours the summary's tint is lost, so it gets a hairline in CanvasText.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <form>, <fieldset> and <legend>. The editor form is named by its heading through aria-labelledby. The error summary is role="alert" with tabindex="-1", named by its heading, and receives focus after a refused submit; each field with an error has aria-invalid="true" and aria-describedby pointing at its message (L2-069.2). The save status is role="status", so its changes are announced politely.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through fields in reading order: down each section, left to right in a field row, then Save changes and Cancel.
EnterIn a text field, submits the form; on a section summary, opens or closes it; on a summary link, moves focus to that field.
SpaceToggles a section summary, a radio or checkbox; activates a button.
EscNo effect on the form. In the discard dialog it means Stay (L2-062.1).

Focus

After a refused save, focus goes to the summary; after a successful save it stays on Save changes. When a field is focused near the bottom, it must not hide behind the sticky bar (WCAG 2.4.11): html:has(.form-actions--sticky) sets a bottom scroll padding as tall as the bar, so the browser scrolls a focused field clear of it.

Labelling

Every field has a visible label. Help sits under the field and is joined by aria-describedby. A field that is only sometimes needed says when in its label: "Provided by (needed when discounted or comped)". Summary links repeat the field's label, so "Light score: Enter a whole number from 1 to 5" makes sense out of context.

Motion

None of its own. Sections open instantly; the Save spinner keeps turning under reduced motion because it reports status.

Touch

All controls are at least 44px tall below 48rem, and every editor control at least 24px at any width (L2-054.6, L2-065). Save changes and Cancel sit --space-3 apart.

Content

  • Summary heading: "Fix n things before saving" in the editors, "Fix n things to sign in" on the sign-in page. Write the number as a numeral.
  • Summary items: "Field label: the field's own message", the message exactly as it appears under the field, capital first letter: "Hourly rate: Enter an amount of 0 or more".
  • Field messages say what to do: "Enter a whole number from 1 to 5", "The date can't be in the future", "Describe this image".
  • Primary button: "Save changes" on an existing item, "Create studio", "Create spot", "Create guide" on a new one, "Sign in" on the sign-in page. Cancel is always "Cancel".
  • Save status: "Not saved yet", "Unsaved changes", "Wait for uploads to finish", "Saving…", "Saved 13 Oct 2026, 10:02 am", "All changes saved", "Not saved. Fix the 6 things listed above."
  • Optional fields say "(optional)" in the label; required is the default and is not marked.

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • Summary headings vary ("Fix 6 things before saving", "Fix 4 problems before saving", "This guide can't be published yet"); the system uses "Fix n things before saving".
  • The spot editor's summary lower-cases each message after the colon; the system copies the field message verbatim.
  • The spot and guide editors (and the sign-in button, "Signing in") swap the busy button's label to "Saving…"; the studio editor keeps "Save changes". The system keeps the label.
  • The refused save status reads "Not saved. Fix the 6 things listed above.", "Not saved" or "Not published"; the new-item status is empty in the studio editor and "Not saved yet" elsewhere. The system uses one string each.
  • .save-status--saved and --error exist in the stylesheet but no mock uses them; the system applies them to the saved and refused states.
  • 32 field rows override grid-template-columns inline (14 always-two, 18 auto-fit); .field-row--pair, .field-row--fit and .field-row--fit-sm now replace them.
  • The sign-in summary has no aria-labelledby and relies on autofocus; it now matches the editors' summary.
  • Nothing stopped the sticky bar covering a focused field at the bottom of the screen (WCAG 2.4.11); html:has(.form-actions--sticky) now adds the bottom scroll padding.
