Chip

Chips are small pills you can act on. On ShotThere they list the filters that are on above the studio and spot results, "Up to $150.00" and "Natural light", each with its own remove button.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable.chip.chip--filter.active-filters

Overview

A chip is a control, not a fact. On ShotThere it has one job: showing which filters are on and letting the visitor drop one without reopening the filter panel.

  • Active filter (.chip--filter): one chip per filter that is on, in the order of the filter panel, inside .active-filters above the results. Each has a remove button; "Clear all" ends the row. Always accent-tinted, because each one is a choice that is on.
  • Toggle (button.chip[aria-pressed]) and static (span.chip) still have CSS but no ShotThere mock uses them yet. They are shown below with filter copy so the states are on record.

Not a chip: the "Best uses" on a spot card (Engagement, Family, Portrait, Brand) and the "Featured in" guide names on a review are tags; "Prices may be out of date" is a badge; a draft or published record is a status label; the filters themselves are checkboxes, radios and number fields in the filter panel; "Both / Studios / Spots" on the map is a segmented control.

Anatomy

  1. LabelThe filter as a short phrase: "Natural light", "Up to $150.00", "Parking: on-site". --text-label in --color-fg-accent.
  2. Remove button.remove: a round <button> with a 16px × icon, named "Remove filter: Natural light". 24 × 24px (--target-min) from 48rem, 44 × 44px below.
  3. ContainerA <span> pill, 36px tall (--control-height-sm) from 48rem and 44px below; --color-accent-subtle fill, 1px --color-accent border, --space-3 left padding and --space-1 right padding so the remove button sits close to the edge.

Sizes

One design size whose height follows the viewport: below 48rem every chip is at least 44px tall and the remove button is 44 × 44px (L2-065.3); from 48rem chips are 36px (--control-height-sm) with a 24px remove button (--target-min), the WCAG 2.5.8 minimum, spaced 8px apart.

  • Selected is the only state an active-filter chip has: it is on until removed. The accent tint matches the checked boxes in the filter panel beside it.
  • Updating: removing a chip updates the URL (L2-025), the list and the polite result count ("5 studios, sorted by all-in cost"). Chips never show spinners; while the list reloads it is marked aria-busy.
  • Disabled exists only for the toggle chip and no mock uses it. An option that would match nothing stays enabled and leads to the empty state.

Responsive behaviour

  • The row wraps (.active-filters is a flex row with --space-2 gaps) and never scrolls sideways. "Clear all" wraps with the chips.
  • The row sits at the top of the results column at every width: under the result count and sort, above the cards. Below 62rem the filter panel is a dialog behind the "Filters" trigger, whose count ("Filters 2") matches the number of chips (L2-022.6).
  • Below 48rem chips and remove buttons grow to 44px (L2-065.3); from 48rem they are 36px and 24px.
  • A long label ("Parking: on-site") stays on one line; the row wraps around it.

Theming

An active-filter chip reads --color-accent-subtle, --color-accent and --color-fg-accent, and --color-accent-subtle-hover under a hovered remove button. A toggle chip reads --color-bg-surface, --color-border-strong and --color-fg-default at rest and the accent set when pressed. No component tokens: a re-skin changes the accent tokens and every "selected" mark moves together. In dark the tint becomes a deep accent with light accent text and border; in forced colours the chip's border takes Highlight so "on" survives.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

An active-filter chip is a plain <span> holding a native remove <button> (APG Button); the chip itself is not focusable. The row starts with visually hidden "Active filters:" so it is introduced once. A toggle chip is a <button aria-pressed> inside a labelled role="group".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabEach remove button is one tab stop, then "Clear all".
Enter, SpaceRemoves that filter.
After removingFocus moves to the next chip's remove button; after the last one, to "Clear all"; when none are left, to the results heading.

Focus

The shared ring draws round the small remove button, offset onto the tint. On a toggle chip it goes round the whole pill.

Labelling

Remove buttons name the filter in full: "Remove filter: Up to $150.00". The × is aria-hidden. The change is announced by the result count, a polite live region (L2-069.3), not by the chip.

Motion

Only the fill transitions, over --duration-fast; reduced motion zeroes it. A removed chip disappears at once; the row reflows without animation.

Touch

Remove buttons are 44 × 44px below 48rem and 24 × 24px from 48rem, with chips 8px apart (L2-065.2, L2-065.3).

Content

  • Write the filter as the visitor set it, in sentence case: "Natural light", "Cyc wall", "Family", "No permit needed".
  • A range names its bound with the full price: "Up to $150.00"; with both bounds, "$90.00 to $150.00".
  • A choice from a named group keeps the group: "Parking: on-site". A neighbourhood is just its name: "Leslieville".
  • The remove button is always "Remove filter: " plus the chip's label.
  • "Clear all" ends the chip row and the filters dialog; the empty state says "Clear filters" (L2-022.4).
