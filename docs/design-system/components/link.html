Link

Links take people somewhere: to a full review, back to the studio picker, to the episode on YouTube, to the park's permit rules. They are ink and underlined, and turn accent red on hover.

Overview

Use a link when activating it only navigates. If it changes something on the page, it is a button, even if it looks like a link ("Clear all" after the active filters is .btn--link).

  • Inline (bare <a>) sits in a sentence: "tell us at corrections@shotthere.ca" on the editorial policy. Ink, underlined, accent red on hover.
  • Standalone (.link--standalone) stands on its own line as the way on from a block: "Read the full review" under each guide entry, "Change studios" above the comparison. Label weight, 44px tall.
  • External (.link--external) leaves ShotThere: "Watch on YouTube" under an episode, "Open in Google Maps" for a spot, a permit source such as "Evergreen: photography and filming". It shows a small arrow, opens a new tab and says so to screen readers.
  • Back link (.eyebrow) is a quiet link above a title. The CSS is kept, but no ShotThere mock uses it: review pages use the breadcrumb ("Studios › The Junction Loft") instead.

A link can be drawn as a button when it is the page's main call to action. "Book on the studio's site" is <a class="btn btn--primary"> with the same external arrow and hidden "(opens in a new tab)" as .link--external: booking happens on the studio's own site, never on ShotThere. Links inside a location card, the comparison table header, the breadcrumb and the footer take those components' styles.

Anatomy

  1. LabelSays where it goes. --color-fg-link (ink); --text-label when standalone, the surrounding text style when inline.
  2. Underline1px (--border-width-hairline) at 0.15em offset; 2px (--border-width-thick) on hover. Always present.
  3. External arrow (external only)The box-and-arrow icon at 0.9em, aria-hidden, after the label; a visually hidden " (opens in a new tab)" follows it.

Sizes

Links take the size of their context. Inline links match the sentence (--text-body, or --text-body-sm in a verified line). Standalone links use --text-label at --font-size-md in a 44px box. External links take their parent's size; the arrow scales with it at 0.9em.

  • Visited is deliberately not styled: a returning visitor comparing studios opens the same reviews again and again, and a faded link would read as unavailable.
  • Active has no separate look; navigation follows at once. Disabled does not exist: a link that cannot go anywhere is plain text.
  • Current page: don't link to the page you are on. The breadcrumb shows the current page as text with aria-current="page"; the site header marks its current section.

Responsive behaviour

  • Inline links wrap with their sentence. A long unbroken string (an email address, a URL) needs overflow-wrap: anywhere on its container so it never forces a sideways scroll at 320px.
  • Standalone links keep their 44px box at every width and wrap their label if it is longer than the column.
  • On review pages the episode's "Watch on YouTube" sits beside the episode title in .episode__links, which wraps under it on phones.
  • An external link's arrow belongs after the last word; keep the label, icon and hidden suffix together with no whitespace between them, so the arrow wraps with the last word instead of on its own.

Theming

Links read --color-fg-link and --color-fg-link-hover; the back link reads --color-fg-muted and --color-fg-default. In dark the link becomes light stone and its hover a light accent red. There are no component tokens; inside an alert, a toast or the footer links inherit that container's colour and keep the underline.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <a href> (APG Link). Never a <span> with a click handler, and never role="link" on a button.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to and from the link in DOM order.
EnterFollows the link. External links open a new tab.

Focus

The shared ring (2px, 3px offset, --color-focus-ring) on :focus-visible, with a small radius so it hugs a wrapped inline link.

Labelling

The text is the name, and it must make sense out of context: "Read the full review" adds the studio in hidden text ("of Kensington Window Room"). External links add " (opens in a new tab)" in hidden text, since the arrow is hidden from assistive technology.

Focus ring

Links are told apart from text by the underline, not by colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1).

Motion

None. The colour and underline change instantly on hover.

Touch

Standalone and back links are at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable). Inline links are exempt from target size because they sit in a sentence (WCAG 2.5.8); keep at least one word between two inline links.

Content

  • Say where it goes, in sentence case: "Read the full review", "Change studios", "Watch on YouTube", "Open in Google Maps". Never "Click here", "More" or a bare URL.
  • Name an external source by its owner and topic: "Evergreen: photography and filming", "City of Toronto: filming and photography in parks", "Distillery District: filming and photography".
  • Show an email address in full as its own text: "corrections@shotthere.ca".
  • A link drawn as a button keeps a verb phrase: "Book on the studio's site", "Browse studios".

Drift fixed during extraction:

  • "Watch on YouTube" carries .link--external and the hidden "(opens in a new tab)" but no arrow in seven mocks (studio/default, playing, in-compare, media-failed; spot/default, map-failed, no-rain-backup), while every other external link shows it. The system always shows the arrow.
  • The map popup's "View the review" is a bare inline <a> on its own line, about 24px tall. As the popup's only way on it should be .link--standalone (44px).
  • The footer's "YouTube channel" opens a new tab with no arrow; the footer page decides its treatment.
  • The external arrow rendered on its own line in the mocks because svg is display: block and nothing in .link--external overrode it. This may be why "Watch on YouTube" dropped the arrow. Fixed in components.css: .link--external .icon is now an inline block after the last word.
