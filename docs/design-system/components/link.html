Link

Links take people somewhere: to a caterer, the privacy notice, a field with an error. They are ink and underlined, and turn pepper on hover.

Overview

Use a link when activating it only goes somewhere: another page, a place on this page, an email address. If it changes something (sends, saves, removes, opens a dialog), use a button, even if it should look like a link (.btn--link, as in "Clear filters"). A link can look like a button ("Browse caterers" on the 404 page uses .btn on an <a>), but it stays a link.

  • Inline (bare <a>) sits in a sentence: "I agree to the privacy notice". Ink, underlined, pepper on hover.
  • Standalone (.link--standalone) stands on its own line or beside a heading: "All enquiries", "Forgot your password?", "View caterer". Label weight, 44px tall, an optional icon.
  • External (.link--external) leaves ShotThere, such as the privacy notice's pointer to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. It shows a small arrow and says so to screen readers.
  • Back link (.eyebrow) is the quiet chevron link above a page title: "Caterers" on a caterer page, "Review queue" on an operator record, "Dashboard" on a setup screen.
  • In context: links inside an alert, the error summary and the footer inherit that container's colour; the underline and wording do the work.

For a list of places to go, use the side navigation, the filter list or the site header; their links have their own current-page styles.

Anatomy

  1. LabelSays where it goes. --color-fg-link (ink); --text-label when standalone, the surrounding text style when inline.
  2. Underline1px (--border-width-hairline) at 0.15em offset; thickens to 2px on hover. Always present on inline links.
  3. Icon (optional)Chevron for back links, arrow for external links; 0.9em on external, .icon--sm on back links; aria-hidden.
  4. TargetStandalone and back links are inline-flex boxes at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable). Inline links take the line height and are exempt from target size.

Sizes

Inline links take the size of their text: --text-body in forms, --text-prose (18px) on the about, privacy and terms pages, --text-body-sm in help and status lines. Standalone links use --text-label at --font-size-md. Back links use --text-body-sm. Every standalone and back link is at least 44px tall, whatever its text size.

Signed in as leah.mohammed@sweethand.example · Not you?

Shown on your listing card. How we use your area

Write to privacy@sweethand.example.

14px status line, 16px body, 18px prose: the link follows its sentence.

  • Visited is deliberately not styled: links are navigation within a small product, and a purple or faded link would break the ink look. Pages that need "seen" use real state instead: inbox rows show unread with a dot and a hidden "Unread." label.
  • Active (pressed) has no separate look; navigation follows immediately. Disabled does not exist: a link that cannot go anywhere is plain text.
  • Current page: don't link to the page you are on. Navigation components (site header, side nav) mark theirs with aria-current="page" and their own style.

Responsive behaviour

  • Inline links wrap with their sentence. Long unbroken strings (email addresses such as kendra@pelauhouse.example in a 320px menu) need overflow-wrap: anywhere on the container, as .account-menu__email already does.
  • Back links with .until-lg show below 62rem only. From LG the side nav (dashboard, operator console) or the list column (inbox) is on screen and gives the same route. Back links without it (caterer page "Caterers", operator record "Review queue") show at every width.
  • Section-head links (.caterer-section__head) wrap under their heading when space runs out, and keep their 44px height.
  • Footer links wrap in rows with --space-6 between them, each 44px tall.

Theming

Links read --color-fg-link (ink: stone-950 in light, stone-100 in dark) and --color-fg-link-hover (pepper-600, pepper-300 in dark). The underline uses --border-width-hairline, then --border-width-thick on hover. Back links and footer links read --color-fg-muted and go to --color-fg-default on hover. In alerts, the error summary and the footer, color: inherit hands the link its container's tone. Hover keeps that colour and only thickens the underline.

Pepper on hover is the one place a link uses the accent. It is momentary, so it never competes with a selected filter. To re-skin, override the two semantic link tokens in a theme; don't add colours to a.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <a href> (APG Link). Never a <span> with a click handler, never an <a> without href. A link that only performs an action is a button.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to and from the link in DOM order.
EnterFollows the link. Space scrolls the page (it does not activate links).
Browser shortcutsOpen in new tab, copy link address: these work because it is a real link.

Focus

The shared 2px pepper ring with a 3px offset and --radius-sm corners. On standalone and back links it wraps the whole 44px box. An error-summary link moves focus to its field (href="#name" with the input's id), so the field's label and error are read next.

Labelling

The link text is the name and makes sense on its own (WCAG 2.4.4): "Read the privacy notice", "Go to photos", "Review the name change". External links add hidden text "(another website)". ShotThere does not open new tabs; if one ever must, say "(opens in a new tab)" too. Don't give a link a title or an aria-label that differs from its visible words.

Inline links are ink like the text around them, so the underline identifies them, not colour (WCAG 1.4.1). Never remove it in running text.

Motion

None at rest; hover changes colour and underline thickness instantly. Smooth scrolling to an in-page target turns off with reduced motion (scroll-behavior: auto).

Touch

Standalone and back links are 44px tall. Inline links in sentences are exempt from WCAG 2.5.8, but don't place two inline links side by side with only a space between them. A link that stands alone in a paragraph ("Forgot your password?") is a standalone link, not an inline one.

Content

  • Say where it goes, in sentence case: "Read the privacy notice", "Go to photos", "View your page", "Upload a new certificate". Never "Click here", "Learn more" or a bare URL.
  • Error-summary links repeat the field's error exactly: "Enter a business name of 2 to 80 characters".
  • Back links name the destination only, because the chevron already says "back": "Caterers", "Dashboard", "Inbox". Not "Back to inbox".
  • Questions are fine when they are the task: "Forgot your password?", "Not you?".
  • Name external organisations in full the first time: "Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada".
