Component · Data display

Badge

Badges are small pills that add one short fact to a value or a name: a fee is mandatory, a permit is not required, a studio is the lowest all-in, its prices may be out of date.

Overview

A badge qualifies the thing it sits next to. It is never interactive and always a word, never a colour alone.

  • Neutral (.badge): a plain qualifier. "Mandatory" and "Optional, not in all-in" beside each fee in a studio's pricing; "Required" in a spot's permit table.
  • Success (.badge--success): good news about a value. "Not required" for a permit; "Lowest" beside the cheapest all-in in the comparison.
  • Warning (.badge--warning): data may be stale. Visitors see "Prices may be out of date" on a studio card, summary panel and comparison column, and "Permit rules may be out of date" on a spot; the admin sees "Prices stale", "Permits stale" and "Stale". Stale means verified more than 183 days ago.
  • Info (.badge--info): a neutral heads-up for the admin. "Due soon" when a verification is 153 to 183 days old.
  • Danger, solid and count (.badge--danger, .badge--solid, .badge--count) keep their CSS but no ShotThere mock uses them yet.

Not a badge: whether a studio or guide is draft or published is a status label; the best uses on a spot card are tags; an active filter is a chip; a long stale-price explanation in the pricing section is a warning alert; who paid for a shoot is the disclosure.

Anatomy

  1. Dot6px circle in the text colour (::before), so the badge reads as a marker even in greyscale. Hidden on solid and count badges.
  2. LabelOne short phrase, --text-label at --font-size-xs, in the tone's -fg colour. It does not wrap by default.
  3. ContainerPill (--radius-full) at least 24px tall (--target-min), --space-2 side padding, tone -bg fill, no border.

Sizes

One size: --font-size-xs label, at least 24px tall (--target-min), --space-2 side padding. It sits on the text baseline beside a value or name and never grows with its container. Count badges are at least 1.375rem wide so single digits stay round.

  • Appears when the server says so: a warning badge shows when a verification date is more than 183 days before today (Monday 13 October 2026, so before 13 Apr 2026); "Due soon" from 153 days. The server computes staleness and the page only shows its flag (L2-043).
  • Goes when the admin saves a new "Prices last verified" date; nothing else clears it.
  • Wrapped: in a 10rem comparison column "Prices may be out of date" is wider than the column, so it must wrap. The mocks do this with an inline white-space: normal; a CSS rule is proposed (see Sources).

Responsive behaviour

  • Badges keep their size at every width. In wrapping rows (.admin-row__title, .kv dd) the badge drops to the next line as a whole beside its value.
  • Below 48rem the permit table stacks (.table--stack): each row becomes "Portrait" over a "Permit" label and its badge.
  • In the comparison the columns scroll sideways under a sticky row label, each about 10rem wide; badges there are allowed to wrap.
  • Below 62rem the studio summary panel moves above the review, so the stale badge is near the top on a phone.

Theming

Each tone reads a feedback pair: --color-success-bg / --color-success-fg, --color-warning-bg / --color-warning-fg, --color-info-bg / --color-info-fg, --color-danger-bg / --color-danger-fg. Neutral reads --color-bg-subtle / --color-fg-muted; solid reads --color-info-solid with --color-bg-surface text; count reads --color-danger-solid with --color-fg-on-danger. Info is a neutral stone, not blue. In dark every tint becomes a deep tone with a light label at the same contrast. No component tokens; re-tone the semantic pair.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain inline text in a <span>; no ARIA role. It is read in place with the value it qualifies: "$25.00 Mandatory", "Portrait, Not required".

Keyboard

KeyAction
NoneBadges are not focusable. A related action ("Edit" on a stale row) is its own button or link.

Focus

Not applicable: a badge never takes focus.

Labelling

The words carry the meaning, the colour only supports them (WCAG 1.4.1). The label must make sense next to its neighbour, so in the comparison "Lowest" sits in the all-in row, whose header is "All-in, 2 hours". A count badge needs hidden text for its noun.

Content

  • Sentence case, two to five words, no full stop: "Mandatory", "Not required", "Lowest", "Due soon".
  • Visitor-facing warnings say what may be wrong, not a system word: "Prices may be out of date", "Permit rules may be out of date". The admin gets terse flags that name the data: "Prices stale", "Permits stale".
  • Pair a warning badge with the verified date nearby ("Prices verified 2 Mar 2026"), so the visitor can judge it.
  • Never use a badge for a record's state ("Draft", "Published") or a fact about the place ("Cyc wall").
