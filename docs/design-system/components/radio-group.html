Radio group

A radio group asks for exactly one answer and keeps every option in view: what parking a studio must have, whether a spot needs a permit for the shoot, and how the session behind a review was paid for.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableNative <input type="radio">.choice

Overview

Use a radio group when exactly one answer applies and the options are few enough to read at a glance (two to five). The mocks use three:

  • Parking in the studio filters: Any, On-site, Street, None (L2-022).
  • Permit in the spot filters: Any, or No permit needed for the selected use (L2-024).
  • Disclosure in the studio and spot editors: "How was this session paid for?" I paid the full rate, Discounted, Comped (free of charge). The answer becomes the disclosure line on the public review (L1-011).

For any number of answers, use checkboxes. For a longer fixed list, or where the form is crowded, use a select. Each option is a label.choice with a round box; the group is a <fieldset> with a <legend>.

Anatomy

  1. Legend<legend>, --text-label, 8px above the first option: the question.
  2. Selected radio20px circle filled --color-accent with an 8px dot in --color-fg-on-accent.
  3. Option label--text-body, 12px after the circle, inside the wrapping label.choice.
  4. Unselected radio1px --color-border-strong ring on --color-bg-surface.

The disclosure in pages/studio-editor/default, where Corktown Garage Studio was paid in full.

Variants

Filter group

Parking
Permit
Parking from the studio filters and Permit from dialogs/spot-filters/selected. "Any" comes first with an empty value and is selected by default, so a filter is never accidentally on and the URL leaves it out (L2-025).

Sizes

One size: a 20px circle beside 16px text. The whole row is the target.

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state; selected, disabled and invalid use checked, disabled and aria-invalid. Pressing (active) shows nothing extra. No mock shows a disabled or invalid radio; those cells are ready for a new studio saved before the disclosure is answered.

Both themes

Nothing chosen yet (invalid)

The new studio and spot editors start with no disclosure answer, because guessing one would publish a false statement. If the owner saves without answering, the error goes under the legend, the fieldset references it, every radio gets aria-invalid="true", and the summary links to the first radio.

How was this session paid for?

Choose how this session was paid for

No mock shows this error yet (pages/studio-editor/new leaves the group empty); the wording follows the editors' "verb, then what is missing".

Responsive behaviour

  • Options stack, one per line, at every width. Long options ("No permit needed for the selected use") wrap under their own text.
  • Below 48rem every row is at least 44px tall (L2-065.3); from 48rem, 24px.
  • In the filters the group sits in the sidebar from LG and in the full-height sheet below it (L2-022.5); it looks the same in both.

Theming

The radio shares the checkbox's tokens: --color-border-strong ring on --color-bg-surface, --color-accent fill with a --color-fg-on-accent dot when selected, --color-danger-solid double ring when invalid. In dark every part moves to its dark value with no extra rules; in forced colours the selected circle takes Highlight.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radios sharing one name, inside a <fieldset> and <legend> (WAI-ARIA APG radio group pattern, built natively). Screen readers hear "How was this session paid for?, group. Discounted, radio button, 2 of 3, selected".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves into the group, to the selected radio (or the first when none is), and out again. The group is one tab stop.
Down / Right, Up / LeftMoves to the next or previous option and selects it, wrapping at the ends.
SpaceSelects the focused option when none is selected yet.

Focus

The shared ring draws round the circle with its offset (2px at 3:1 or more, L2-067.2). Choosing Discounted or Comped enables "Provided by" but doesn't move focus to it.

Labelling

The legend is the question; each option's text is its name. A dependent field outside the group names its condition in its label ("needed when discounted or comped"), so it makes sense when reached on its own.

Motion

Fill and ring colour transition over --duration-fast; reduced motion zeroes it. The dot appears at once.

Touch

The whole row is the target: 44px tall below 48rem and at least 24px above (L2-065.2–3).

Content

  • The legend asks the question in the owner's words: "How was this session paid for?"
  • Options are short, parallel and mutually exclusive: Any, On-site, Street, None.
  • Filters start with "Any" so the default never hides results. "Permit" is a two-option group rather than a checkbox because L2-024 offers it as a choice between "no permit needed" and "any"; keep "Any" first.
  • Option words match what the public page shows, or the label says what visitors will see: "I paid the full rate" becomes "Paid in full".
  • Brackets explain, they don't qualify: "Comped (free of charge)".
