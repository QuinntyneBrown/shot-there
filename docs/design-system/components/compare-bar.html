Compare toggle and bar

The compare toggle adds a studio to a comparison from its card or its review page. The compare bar, fixed to the bottom of the screen, counts the selection and opens the comparison once two to four studios are picked (L2-030).

Overview

Comparing is how ShotThere pays off its one rule, the same measurements for every studio. The toggle and the bar are the two halves of picking what to compare; the comparison table is where it ends.

  • Compare toggle: a button with aria-pressed, on every studio card in the studio list ("Compare" / "Remove") and in the summary panel of a studio review ("Add to compare" / "Remove from compare").
  • Compare bar: appears as soon as one studio is picked, stays fixed to the bottom of the viewport on every page that has a toggle, and holds the count, the picked names, Clear and Compare.
  • Studios only. Outdoor spots are not compared, so spot cards and spot pages have no toggle.
  • Two to four studios. With one picked, Compare is unavailable and a hint says why (L2-030.2); a fifth is refused with a warning toast (L2-030.3).
  • The selection is kept in the browser, so it survives a reload and a visit to a studio page (L2-030.5). There are no accounts, so it is never sent anywhere until Compare opens /compare?studios=….

Anatomy

Toggle

  1. Visible labelThe action: "Compare" when off, "Remove" when on (L2-030.1). On the review page, "Add to compare" and "Remove from compare".
  2. Hidden studio nameA .visually-hidden span after the label in cards, so each toggle has a unique name ("Remove The Junction Loft").
  3. ContainerA .btn (small in cards). Pressed swaps its component tokens to the accent-subtle fill, accent text and accent edge.

Bar

  1. Count.compare-bar__count: "1 selected", in a polite live region, so every change is announced.
  2. Names.compare-bar__names: the picked studios in the order they were added, on one line with an ellipsis.
  3. Hint (one studio only).compare-bar__hint: "Pick at least 2 studios to compare", referenced by the Compare button.
  4. ClearGhost button; empties the selection and removes the bar.
  5. ComparePrimary, always last. A link to /compare?studios=… from 2 picked; an aria-disabled button at 1.
  6. Surface.compare-bar: raised surface, hairline top edge, --shadow-3, fixed to the bottom with the safe-area inset added to its padding.

Sizes

The toggle takes its size from where it sits: small (.btn--sm, 36px, 44px below 48rem) in a card, medium (44px) in the review page's summary panel beside "Book on the studio's site". The bar has one size: its height is its content (one or two text lines plus 44px buttons) with --space-3 padding above and below.

Responsive behaviour

  • Every width: the bar is fixed to the bottom of the viewport (L2-030.6). Its inner row is capped at the container width and centred, so on a wide screen the count lines up with the page's left edge and Compare with its right edge.
  • Safe area: the bottom padding is --space-3 plus env(safe-area-inset-bottom), so the buttons clear a phone's home indicator (L2-065.1). Pages need viewport-fit=cover for the inset to be reported.
  • Phones: the status takes a 12rem basis; when status and actions do not fit on one line the actions wrap below it, still in Clear-then-Compare order. Names never wrap: they cut off with an ellipsis, and the count stays complete.
  • End of page: the page adds .has-compare-bar to <body>, which pads its end by --space-24 (96px) plus the inset so the footer can scroll clear of the bar. Measured at 360px, the bar is 143px tall with one studio (count, name and hint over wrapped buttons) and 122px with two to four, so below 36rem .has-compare-bar pads by --space-32 plus --space-8 (160px) plus the inset and the footer clears the bar.
  • Toggle: the card toggle is 36px from 48rem and 44px below it; the review-page toggle is 44px everywhere.

Theming

The toggle is a .btn; .compare-toggle[aria-pressed="true"] only re-points the button's four component tokens, so it themes with the button and with nothing else:

.compare-toggle[aria-pressed="true"] {
  --btn-bg: var(--color-accent-subtle);
  --btn-fg: var(--color-fg-accent);
  --btn-border: var(--color-accent);
  --btn-bg-hover: var(--color-accent-subtle-hover);
}

The bar reads --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default and --shadow-3. In the dark theme the raised surface is lighter than the canvas and the shadow is deeper, so the bar still reads as sitting above the page. The accent stays red in both themes; the primary Compare button is the ink action colour, which flips to near-white in dark.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

  • Toggle: native <button type="button"> with aria-pressed, the WAI-ARIA APG Button pattern's toggle button. L2-030.1 also changes the visible label ("Compare" to "Remove"), so a screen reader hears "Remove The Junction Loft, toggle button, pressed". The APG recommends a label that does not change with the state; the spec's label change is recorded as an open question, and until it is answered both the label and aria-pressed stay, because the pressed styling hangs on the attribute.
  • Bar: <aside aria-label="Studios to compare">, a complementary landmark, so it can be reached from the landmarks list. It sits after <main> and before the footer in the DOM, which is also its tab position.
  • Count: role="status" aria-live="polite" announces "2 selected" after each change without moving focus.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCards' name links and toggles in reading order, then the bar's Clear and Compare, then the footer.
Enter, SpaceOn a toggle: adds or removes the studio; focus stays on the toggle. On Clear: empties the selection. On Compare (a link): Enter opens the comparison.
EscNo effect on the bar; it is not a dialog. Esc dismisses a limit toast that has focus.

Focus

  • Toggling never moves focus; the count announces the change. Over the limit, focus stays on the pressed button and the toast announces itself.
  • Clear removes the bar, so focus would be left on a removed element: move it to <main> (which has tabindex="-1" on every page).
  • Focus not obscured (2.4.11): the fixed bar can cover a focused footer link when tabbing down the page. html:has(body.has-compare-bar) sets a bottom scroll padding as tall as the bar on its two-row phone layout, so the browser scrolls a focused element clear of it.
  • The ring is the shared one: 2px --color-focus-ring, 2px offset, on the toggle and on both bar buttons.

Labelling

  • Card toggles: visible verb plus visually hidden studio name, so every toggle in a list is distinct.
  • Review-page toggle: the visible label only; the page's h1 is the studio name.
  • Compare with one studio: aria-disabled="true" keeps the button focusable so its aria-describedby hint is read ("Compare, dimmed, Pick at least 2 studios to compare"); activation does nothing.

The bar's top edge (--color-border-default) is decorative; the shadow and the raised surface separate it from the page.

Motion

The bar appears and disappears without animation. Toggles use the button's --duration-fast colour transition and 1px press, both removed under prefers-reduced-motion. The limit toast slides up over --duration-base, which reduced motion zeroes.

Touch

Card toggles are 44px tall on phones, bar buttons 44px at every width, with 8px between Clear and Compare. The toggle sits above the card's stretched link, so tapping it never opens the review.

Content

  • Card toggle: "Compare" off, "Remove" on, each followed by the hidden studio name. Review-page toggle: "Add to compare", "Remove from compare".
  • Count: "n selected", a numeral, no "studios" (the bar's label says what is counted).
  • Names: the studios' published names in the order they were picked, comma separated, no "and".
  • Hint at one: "Pick at least 2 studios to compare". Nothing else is added below the count at two to four.
  • Actions: "Clear" and "Compare". Not "Reset", not "Compare now".
  • Limit toast: title "You can compare up to 4 studios", text "Remove one from the bar to add Studio name." It names the studio that was refused.

Drift found during extraction. The first two need the mocks changed; the CSS ones are fixed in components.css:

  • pages/studio/in-compare: Compare comes before Clear (every other mock has Clear, then Compare); the bar is labelled "Comparison" instead of "Studios to compare"; and .has-compare-bar sits on <main>, so the footer after it is not padded. Resolution: Clear then Compare, one label, the class on <body>.
  • pages/studios/selected-one: Compare is a native disabled button with aria-describedby, which a keyboard user can never reach. Resolution: aria-disabled="true" (same look, through .btn[aria-disabled="true"]) and ignore activation.
  • End padding too short on phones. At 360px the bar is 143px (one picked) and 122px (two to four) against 96px of padding from .has-compare-bar, so the end of the footer stayed under the bar (L2-030.6). Fixed: @media (max-width: 35.99rem) { .has-compare-bar { padding-bottom: calc(var(--space-32) + var(--space-8) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)); } }.
  • Focus hidden behind the bar. Nothing reserved the bar's height when the browser scrolled a focused element into view (WCAG 2.4.11). Fixed: html:has(body.has-compare-bar) { scroll-padding-bottom: calc(var(--space-32) + var(--space-8) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)); }.
  • Toast offset inline. notifications/public-toast/warning lifts the toast region above the bar with style="bottom: calc(var(--space-24) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom));". Fixed: .has-compare-bar .toast-region { bottom: calc(var(--space-24) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)); }.
  • Toggle label changes while aria-pressed also changes (L2-030.1 and every mock). Recorded as an open question; see Accessibility.
