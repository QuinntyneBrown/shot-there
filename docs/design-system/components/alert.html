Alert

An alert is a tinted block that stays as long as something is true: these prices may be out of date, your session expired, too many sign-in attempts, we couldn't load the re-verification list. It sits inside the content it is about.

Overview

Use an alert for a condition on the page that the reader should know before acting: stale data on a public page, the outcome of a sign-in, a failure inside a dialog, a draft just created. It is placed next to what it is about (under the pricing, above the sign-in form, inside the dialog body) and is not dismissed on a timer.

  • For a note about one field, use an inline message. A failed image upload is one: it sits in the image row, not in an alert.
  • For a confirmation of an action that just finished (published, unpublished, link copied, save failed), use a toast.
  • For a page that could not load at all, use an empty state or the error page.
  • For missing publish fields, the Can't publish yet dialog lists them; a refused save opens the form summary.

Anatomy

  1. Tone icon--icon-size-md, aria-hidden, in --alert-icon. Its shape (circle i, tick, triangle, circle !) repeats the tone without colour.
  2. Title (optional)p.alert__title, --text-label at --font-size-md: the condition in a few words.
  3. BodyOne or two sentences: what it means and what to do.
  4. Actions (optional).alert__actions: one small button or one link; inherits the alert's ink.
  5. Container.alert: tone fill and 1px tone border, --radius-md, --space-4 padding. A dismiss button (.close) may sit at the end.

Variants

Four tones. The tone is the meaning; pick it from what happened, not from how loud it should be.

Warning: stale data on a public page

Prices may be out of date

We last checked these prices 225 days ago. Confirm with the studio before you book.

Permit rules may be out of date

We last checked these rules 207 days ago. Confirm with the Distillery District before you shoot.

Ossington Blackbox (verified 2 Mar 2026) under its pricing; Distillery District (20 Mar 2026) under its permit table. Shown only past 183 days (L2-005.2, L2-018.3); announced as a status.

Info: session and comparison links

Your session expired

Sign in again to carry on where you were.

You can compare up to 4 studios. We're showing the first 4 from this link.

One studio in this link couldn't be found, so we're comparing the other two.

Above the sign-in form after a timeout (L2-051); above the comparison table when a shared link has five studios or an unknown slug (L2-033). A one-sentence alert needs no title.

Success: signed out and draft created

You've signed out.

Your session has ended on this device.

Go to the public site

Studio created as a draft

Its address will be /studios/corktown-garage-studio.

The sign-in page after Sign out, with one link; the editor after the first save of a new studio, dismissible because the draft is already visible below it.

Warning: rate limited

Too many sign-in attempts

Wait 15 minutes, then try again.

Too many tries.

Wait a minute, then try again.

Owner sign-in (L2-049) and the email signup form (L2-039). The request was refused, so it is announced at once with role="alert".

Danger: refused or failed

Email or password is incorrect

Check both and try again. Passwords are case-sensitive.

We couldn't subscribe you just now.

Your email is still here — try again.

We couldn't unpublish it. Nothing changed. Try again.

Wrong credentials (L2-049), a signup that failed on the server, and a failed Unpublish inside its dialog, where the footer's Unpublish button is the retry.

Inside a dialog

A published studio needs at least one standard test shot. Add another before removing this one.

The Remove image dialog when the last test shot is refused (L2-058). The alert is the dialog's description (aria-describedby), so it has no live role of its own.

Sizes

One size. Alerts take the width of the column they sit in: the review column, the sign-in card or the dialog body.

Responsive behaviour

  • The alert fills its column at every width; icon and dismiss keep their size and the body wraps between them. Long addresses such as /studios/corktown-garage-studio break anywhere.
  • Actions wrap onto a new line under the body; a small button in an alert is 44px tall below 48rem, and the dismiss button grows to 44 × 44 (L2-065.1).
  • On the studio page the stale warning stays in the pricing section at every width; the summary panel repeats it as a badge, which moves above the long sections below 62rem.

Theming

Each tone modifier sets four component tokens from the status roles: --alert-bg (--color-*-bg), --alert-fg (--color-*-fg), --alert-border (--color-*-border) and --alert-icon (--color-*-icon). The dark theme darkens the fill and lightens the ink and icon, keeping 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for the icon. In forced colours the fill vanishes and the 1px border and icon shape remain.

.alert--warning { --alert-bg: var(--color-warning-bg); --alert-fg: var(--color-warning-fg);
                  --alert-border: var(--color-warning-border); --alert-icon: var(--color-warning-icon); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

WAI-ARIA APG Alert pattern and WCAG 4.1.3 Status Messages. The live role follows urgency, not tone:

  • role="status" (polite) for conditions found when the page loads or after a successful step: stale prices and permits, session expired, signed out, comparison link trimmed, draft created.
  • role="alert" (assertive) for a request the person just made that was refused or failed: incorrect sign-in, rate limited, signup failed, dashboard list failed, unpublish or delete failed.
  • No role when the alert is a dialog's description (Remove image refused); the dialog announces it through aria-describedby.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the action and the dismiss button in DOM order. The alert itself is not focusable.
Enter / SpaceActivates Try again or Dismiss; Enter follows the link action.

Focus

An alert never steals focus. On sign-in pages focus goes to the email field (autofocus) while the alert is announced. After Dismiss, focus moves to the next heading so it is not lost.

Labelling

The title and body are the content; the icon is aria-hidden and the tone is in the words ("may be out of date", "couldn't", "incorrect"), never in colour alone. Dismiss has aria-label="Dismiss".

Motion

None. Alerts appear with the content; no slide or fade.

Touch

Content

  • Title: the condition in sentence case, a few words: "Prices may be out of date", "Your session expired", "Too many sign-in attempts".
  • Body: what it means and the next step, with the real number: "We last checked these prices 225 days ago. Confirm with the studio before you book."
  • Say "we" for ShotThere's side of a failure ("We couldn't sign you in just now") and reassure about what is kept ("What you typed is still here", "Nothing changed").
  • Name waits exactly: "Wait 15 minutes, then try again."
  • One action at most, as a verb: "Try again", "Go to the public site".
  • Drop the title when the alert is one sentence ("You can compare up to 4 studios. We're showing the first 4 from this link.").
