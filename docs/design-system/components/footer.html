Footer

The end of every public page. It repeats the promise (nothing is listed unless it has been shot there), offers new reviews by email, links to the lists, the editorial policy and the YouTube channel, and says plainly that nobody pays for placement.

Overview

.site-footer closes every public page: home, the studio and spot lists, every review, compare, the guides, the editorial policy and the 404 and server-error pages. It is quiet: canvas background, muted small text, no border, and --space-24 of air above it.

  • Brand: "ShotThere" and the one-line promise.
  • Email signup: the compact form of the email signup, "New reviews by email" with a secondary Subscribe button. Its invalid, sending, done and rate-limited states are documented on that page (L2-039, L2-041).
  • Links: Studios, Spots, Guides, Editorial policy, and the YouTube channel, which opens in a new tab. Map and Compare are left out; the header has them.
  • Copy line: "© 2026 ShotThere · No paid placement: order comes only from the sort you choose."

Admin screens and the owner sign-in have no footer: the owner needs the editor's sticky actions at the bottom, not a newsletter form. The map page has none either, because the map fills the screen.

Anatomy

  1. Brand.site-footer__brand: the name in --text-h4 and --color-fg-default, the promise in muted --text-body-sm; at most 22rem wide.
  2. Email signupA .signup form labelled by its .signup__title heading, with one email field, a secondary Subscribe button and the hidden .signup__trap honeypot. Grows to fill the row (flex: 1 1 20rem).
  3. Footer links.site-footer__links, a <nav aria-label="Footer"> around a plain list. Muted text, no underline until hover, each 44px tall. Stacked at XS, one row from 36rem.
  4. Copy line.site-footer__copy, full width (flex-basis: 100%) on its own line at the end.

Variants

One footer, identical on every public page. The only thing that changes is the current link: on the studio and spot lists the matching footer link carries aria-current="page", like the header.

The signup in the footer always starts empty. When it is submitted, the result replaces the form in place as on the home page; see Email signup for the states.

Sizes

One size. Its padding (--space-10 top and bottom, --layout-margin at the sides) and width (--layout-container-max) follow the page container, so it lines up with the content above it at every breakpoint.

States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state; current uses the real aria-current="page" and disabled the real disabled.

Responsive behaviour

  • XS (below 36rem): one column. Brand, then the signup with the field and a full-width Subscribe stacked (L2-039.5), then the links one per line, then the copy line.
  • SM and up (36rem): the signup field and button share a row, and the links run in one row with --space-6 between them, wrapping when they run out of room.
  • MD and up: brand and signup sit side by side (flex-wrap with justify-content: space-between); the links and the copy line follow underneath.
  • The bottom padding adds env(safe-area-inset-bottom); on pages with the compare bar, .has-compare-bar on <body> adds room so the bar never covers the copy line (L2-065.1).
  • Nothing scrolls sideways at 320px: the brand and signup shrink, long words wrap (L2-064.1).

Theming

The footer reads semantic tokens only: --color-fg-muted for text and links, --color-fg-default for the name, a hovered link and the current link, and the page's --color-bg-canvas behind it (it has no surface of its own). It has no component tokens. The Subscribe button and the email field theme with Button and Text field. In dark it stays on the canvas; there is no rule or band to separate it, the --space-24 gap does that.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<footer> as a direct child of <body> is the content-info landmark. The links are a <nav aria-label="Footer"> around a <ul>; the signup is a native <form> named by its heading through aria-labelledby.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabEmail field, Subscribe, then each footer link in order. The honeypot field is skipped (tabindex="-1").
EnterIn the email field, submits the signup; on a link, follows it.
SpaceOn Subscribe, submits.

Focus

The shared 2px --color-focus-ring with a 3px offset. After a submit, focus stays on the form and the result is announced by the signup's live region; it never jumps to the top of the page.

Labelling

The email field has a visible "Email address" label. The YouTube link adds " (opens in a new tab)" as visually hidden text, so its name warns about the new tab. The honeypot sits in an aria-hidden="true" wrapper with its own label, so it is labelled for validators and invisible to assistive technology (L2-040.4).

Contrast

Links, promise and copy line on canvas
Name, hovered and current link on canvas
Email field and Subscribe edges on canvas
Focus ring on canvas

Hover adds an underline as well as the darker ink, and current adds weight, so neither relies on colour alone.

Motion

None of its own. Links change colour instantly; the Subscribe spinner follows Button.

Touch

Every footer link is at least 44px tall (--target-comfortable), and the stacked links at XS each take a full row. The email field and Subscribe are 44px (--control-height-md).

Content

  • The promise is the product's one-line description, word for word: "Toronto photo studios and outdoor spots, reviewed first-hand. Nothing is listed unless it has been shot there."
  • Signup: title "New reviews by email", line "One email when a new studio or spot goes up. Unsubscribe any time.", button "Subscribe".
  • Links in this order: Studios, Spots, Guides, Editorial policy, YouTube channel.
  • The copy line keeps the year and the no-paid-placement promise together: "© 2026 ShotThere · No paid placement: order comes only from the sort you choose."
  • No social icons, no "Advertise with us", no partner logos: they would contradict the copy line.
