Popover

A small raised panel that opens from a trigger and stays beside it, without blocking the rest of the page. On ShotThere it shows a map marker's name, its key fact and the way to its page.

Overview

Use a popover when a trigger needs to reveal a few lines of detail and, at most, a link or two, while the page behind stays usable. The one place ShotThere does this is the map: activating a marker opens a popup with the location's kind, name and key fact (all-in cost for a studio, the portrait permit for a spot) and a link to its page (L2-028). That popup is .popover.map-popup; the Map page documents the marker, the placement over the marker and the phone dock. This page documents the primitive underneath.

  • Use a dialog when the task needs the visitor's full attention, a decision or any input. A popover never holds a form.
  • Use a sheet or drawer for long content on phones, such as the studio filters.
  • There is no tooltip in this system. If a label needs explaining, put the explanation on the page.

Anatomy

  1. Panel.popover: --color-bg-surface-raised, hairline --color-border-default, --radius-md, --shadow-3, 16px padding, up to 20rem wide. role="dialog", not modal.
  2. Title (optional).popover__title: label type at 16px; it names the dialog through aria-labelledby.
  3. BodyOne or two short lines. Plain text classes; no component of its own.
  4. Actions (optional).popover__actions: small buttons or links, right-aligned, 8px apart, 12px below the body.

Variants

The primitive is a panel; what changes is whether it holds controls, because that decides where focus goes. .popover is absolutely positioned at --z-popover and scales in; .popover--static puts it in the flow with no animation, for documentation and for any surface that lays it out itself.

Sizes

The popover has one size: it fits its content up to min(20rem, 90vw) with --space-4 padding. The map popup sets its own width of min(17rem, 100vw − 2 × --layout-margin).

States

The popover is either shown or absent; the states live on its trigger. Hover and focus are forced with data-state; open is the real aria-expanded="true". These are the map's marker triggers, the only popover triggers in the mocks.

Responsive behaviour

  • The panel never grows past min(20rem, 90vw), so at 360px it still leaves the page margin visible on both sides and never causes a sideways scroll.
  • The map popup does more: from 48rem it floats above its marker; below 48rem it docks as a card along the bottom of the map (.map-popup--dock), so it never slides off a narrow screen and the map above it stays in view. See Map.
  • Buttons in .popover__actions are small on desktop and grow to 44px below 48rem (L2-065.1).

Theming

The panel reads --color-bg-surface-raised, --color-border-default, --shadow-3 and --radius-md; its text uses the normal foreground roles. In the light theme the raised surface is white like a card, so the shadow and hairline separate it from the page; in the dark theme the raised surface is a step lighter than the canvas and the shadow deepens, so the panel still reads as lifted over the dark map tiles. Composite components (the map popup) add their own type and placement but never their own colours. In forced colours the panel keeps its border, drawn in CanvasText by the system.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A non-modal dialog: the panel is role="dialog" with aria-labelledby pointing at its title and no aria-modal, because the map and list stay usable behind it. The trigger is a real <button> with aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-expanded and, while open, aria-controls, following the APG Dialog labelling rules and the disclosure-style trigger state. The page is not made inert.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches each trigger (every marker is focusable, L2-028.3). Inside an open popover, moves through its controls and then on to the next trigger.
Enter, SpaceOn the trigger: opens the popover, or closes it when it is open.
EscCloses the popover from the trigger or from inside it, and returns focus to the trigger (L2-028.2).
Pointer outsideCloses the popover without moving focus.

Focus

Focus moves in only when the popover holds controls. The map popup holds a link and a Close button, so opening it moves focus to "View the review"; a title-and-text popover leaves focus on its trigger. Closing with Esc or Close always returns focus to the trigger. The panel follows its trigger in the DOM so that Tab from its last control lands on the next trigger, not at the end of the page. Controls inside show the shared 2px --color-focus-ring.

Labelling

The title names the dialog ("The Junction Loft"); its heading level follows the page outline (an <h2> on the Map page, where the popup is a top-level region). The trigger's name says what it is ("The Junction Loft, studio"). An icon-only Close carries aria-label="Close". A link that repeats across popovers gets the location's name in visually hidden text: "View the review: The Junction Loft" reads "View the review: The Junction Loft".

The panel's hairline border is decorative: the shadow and the surface step carry the edge.

Motion

scale-in (96% to 100% with a fade) over --duration-fast and --ease-enter. Reduced motion zeroes the duration, so the panel simply appears. .popover--static never animates.

Touch

Markers are 44px targets whatever the size of the dot. Nothing opens on hover: a tap or click opens the popover, so it works the same with touch, pen and keyboard. The map popup's Close is a 44px icon button.

Content

  • Title: the thing the trigger points at, by its published name: "The Junction Loft", "Evergreen Brick Works".
  • Body: the one fact that helps decide whether to go further, as on the cards: "All-in for 2 hours: $145.00" for a studio, "Portraits: no permit needed" for a spot.
  • Actions: verb phrases naming the destination: "View the review" for a studio, "View the spot" for a spot. One action is usual; two is the maximum.
  • No paragraphs, lists or images. If it needs more than three short lines, it belongs on the page or in a dialog.
