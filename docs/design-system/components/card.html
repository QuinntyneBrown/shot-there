Component · Containers and overlays

Card

A card is a bordered surface that groups one thing: a studio, a count, a block of facts. ShotThere screens use specialised cards (location card, stat tile, summary panel); .card is the generic surface they share, kept for anything new that needs a box.

Overview

No ShotThere mock puts .card on a product surface (a search of all 143 mocks finds it only in the mock gallery's own index page). Every boxed surface on the site is a specialised component with its own page:

  • Location card (.location-card): a studio or spot in the lists, the home page, the guides and the map list. Photo, name link, neighbourhood and review date, two facts, the compare toggle. It is borderless: the photo is the edge.
  • Stat tile (.stat-tile): Studios, Spots and Guides on the admin dashboard, with their published and draft counts.
  • Summary panel (.summary-panel): all-in cost, overall score and Add to compare beside a review; documented on review layout. The editors' status aside (.editor-aside) is on editor layout.

Use .card when a new surface groups related content and none of those fit. Use .well for a sunken, borderless box inside a surface (a note, a test setup). Don't use a card to make a single paragraph look important; use an alert or plain text.

Anatomy

  1. Surface.card: --color-bg-surface, 1px --color-border-default hairline, --radius-lg, --space-6 padding. No shadow at rest.
  2. Title.card__title in --text-h4; on a page it is a heading at the right level (h2 or h3).
  3. Header end (optional).card__header pushes a badge or one small action to the end, --space-4 above the body.
  4. BodyAny content: text, a description list, a table in a flush card.
  5. Footer (optional).card__footer: hairline above, actions right-aligned and wrapping, --space-3 apart.

.card--selected (or data-state="selected") draws an accent-red edge. It only mirrors a real control inside the card, here the compare toggle with aria-pressed="true"; the card never carries the state alone. The shipped location cards show selection on the toggle only and do not use this edge.

Well

Test setup

One Godox AD600 in a 120 cm octabox at 45°, 1.5 m from the subject, f/8, 1/160 s, ISO 100, 85 mm.

.well: --color-bg-surface-sunken, --radius-md, --space-4 padding, no border. It sits inside a page or card for a quiet aside. The fee rows in the studio editor (.fee-row) and the disclosure callout use the same sunken surface with their own classes.

Sizes

One size. Padding is --space-6 (24px) at every width, or 0 for .card--flush; the width is whatever the grid or container gives it.

States

A static card has no states. An interactive card shows hover and focus; a selectable card shows selected. Cards are never disabled: a card whose action is unavailable leaves the action out and says why.

In dark the surface is one step lighter than the canvas and the hairline is --palette-stone-750, so a card still reads as raised without a shadow.

Responsive behaviour

  • A card fills the width it is given; grids decide how many sit in a row (.grid--auto fits 18rem tracks, collapsing to one column on phones without a media query).
  • .card__header keeps the title and badge on one row; a long title wraps beside the badge rather than pushing it out. .card__footer wraps its buttons, and they grow to 44px tall below 48rem.
  • Padding stays --space-6 on phones: at 320px a card inside a 16px margin still leaves a 240px content box.

Theming

The card reads semantic tokens only: --color-bg-surface, --color-border-default, --color-border-strong on hover, --color-accent when selected and --shadow-2 on hover. The well reads --color-bg-surface-sunken. Both themes redefine those tokens; the card has no theme-specific rules. In forced colours the card keeps a 1px CanvasText edge.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A card is not a widget and has no ARIA pattern. Use <article> when it stands alone (a guide teaser), <li> inside a list of cards, otherwise <div>. An interactive card has exactly one real link or button (the title link); the card element itself is never the control, never has tabindex and never role="button".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves to the title link, then to any other control in the card, in reading order.
EnterFollows the title link.

Focus

On an interactive card the link's own ring is hidden and the whole card shows the shared ring through :focus-within (2px --color-focus-ring, 3px offset), so the focused target is as big as the clickable one.

Labelling

The title link's text is the card's accessible name; keep facts out of the link so a screen-reader list of links reads "Best natural light", not a paragraph. Other controls inside name their object: a visible "Edit prices" or "Compare" plus a visually hidden studio name, as the mocks do.

The resting hairline (--color-border-default) is decorative and exempt from 1.4.11: the card's content, not its edge, tells you what it is. The selected edge carries meaning, so it meets 3:1 and the control inside says the same in text.

Motion

Hover transitions shadow, edge and a 1px lift over --duration-fast. Reduced motion removes the lift; the shadow change stays because it is not movement.

Touch

The stretched link makes the whole card the target, far above 44px. Controls inside a card sit above the stretched link (position: relative) and keep their own 44px targets on phones.

Content

  • The title is the thing's name: "Liberty Cyc House", "Best natural light". Not "Studio details".
  • Meta lines are facts separated by middots, with dates as 2 Mar 2026 and money as $145.00: "Studio · Prices verified 2 Mar 2026 (225 days ago)".
  • One badge at most in the header, and only for a state the reader must act on: "Stale", "Due soon", "Draft".
  • Footer actions are verb phrases that name the object: "Edit prices", not "More".

Sources

grep 'class="card' over docs/mocks/pages, dialogs and notifications finds no use of .card or .well; the only match is the mock gallery (mocks/index), which styles its own preview tiles. The card stays as a core component. The specialised surfaces it informs come from:

  • pages/studios/default, pages/home/default, pages/guides/default (.location-card, stretched title link)
  • pages/admin-dashboard/default (.stat-tile; the re-verification copy used above)
  • pages/studio/default (.summary-panel; the test setup used in the well)
  • pages/studio-editor/default (.editor-aside, .fee-row)
  • pages/guide/default (guide names and summaries)

Drift found during extraction (recorded, mocks unchanged): .stat-tile, .summary-panel, .editor-aside and .editor-section each restate the card's hairline and --radius-lg with their own padding (20/24px, 24px, 20px) instead of building on .card. The interactive card has no data-state="focus" hook, so the documentation draws that cell inline.
