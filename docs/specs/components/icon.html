Icon sheet

Every IconName, drawn from the table in icon.md, shown at lg so the strokes are easy to check. Names marked ICD 1.1.0 are the seven additive members (D-1); names marked drawn have no design-system drawing (D-2). AC-1, AC-2

Sizes

smmdlgxl
sm 16 px (.icon--sm), md 20 px (no modifier), lg 24 px (.icon--lg), xl 28 px (.icon--xl). lg and xl use specimen rules: the ICD names them but components.css does not define them yet.

In context

The context rules (icon.md › Design) size and colour the icon by its parent. The rendered SVG is always aria-hidden="true".

Responsive

The icon does not change by breakpoint; its size is in rem or em and scales with text. One phone frame shows nothing overflows at 320 px.
