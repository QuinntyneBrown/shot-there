States

Hover and focus are forced with data-state (documentation only); invalid and disabled use aria-invalid and disabled. Open is the platform's own list and is not drawn.

Responsive

The select has no breakpoint rule of its own: it fills its column, and the inline sort keeps its natural width and wraps under "Sort by" when the row is too narrow. Rows of selects are the application's layout (one per row on a phone, four across from about 40rem). Each frame is a real viewport.
