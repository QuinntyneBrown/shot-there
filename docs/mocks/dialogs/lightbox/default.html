Who it's for

Portrait, headshot and small product shoots that want big, soft window light in the afternoon, and anyone who would rather not rent lights: two strobes and modifiers are in the rate.

Who should skip it

Crews that need a cyc wall, full-length fashion with a large set, or easy load-in for heavy gear: the freight elevator is narrow and booked through the front desk.
