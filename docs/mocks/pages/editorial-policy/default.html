Editorial policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

ShotThere reviews places to shoot photos in Toronto. These are the rules every review follows.

We only list places we've shot

Nothing is listed unless I've booked it or visited it and shot there myself. No listings from press releases, owner submissions or photos someone else took.

Scores are never for sale

No studio, spot or brand can pay for a score, a better position in a list, or a place in a guide. Lists are ordered only by the sort you choose.

We tell you how each session was paid for

Every review carries one of three statements, next to the verdict:

  • Paid in full: “I paid the full rate for this session.”
  • Discounted: “This session was discounted by the studio. That did not affect the scores.” The studio is named.
  • Comped: “This session was provided free of charge by the studio. That did not affect the scores.” The studio is named.

Outdoor spots say “Shot on a regular visit; no one paid for or arranged this review.”

Owners can correct facts, not reviews

If a price, a measurement or an address is wrong, tell us at corrections@shotthere.ca and we'll check it and fix it. Owners can't edit, approve or remove a review, a score or a verdict.

We publish the bad with the good

Scuffed cycs, tiny elevators, streetcar rumble and stale prices go in the review. A low score stays up.

How we test

Every studio gets the same measurements: usable space and ceiling height with a laser measure, window direction with a compass, and natural light at four times of day. Every studio gets the same three test shots with the same setup: one Godox AD600 in a 120 cm octabox at 45°, 1.5 m from the subject, f/8, 1/160 s, ISO 100, 85 mm. Prices are re-checked and dated; anything older than six months is flagged.

No paid placement

There are no sponsored, promoted or featured slots anywhere on the site, and there never will be.
