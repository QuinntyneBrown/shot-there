Editorial policy

How ShotThere reviews studios and outdoor spots, and what we will never do.

We only list places we have shot at

Every studio and outdoor spot on ShotThere has been shot at by our reviewer, with the same measurements and, for studios, the same three test setups. If we haven't shot there, it isn't listed.

Scores are never for sale

No studio can pay for a score, a better position or a mention. There is no sponsored or promoted placement anywhere on the site. Lists are ordered only by the sort you choose.

Comped sessions are disclosed

Every review says whether we paid the full rate, got a discount or were given the session free, and by whom. Payment never changes a score.

Owners can correct facts, not reviews

If a price, address or specification is wrong, a studio owner can tell us and we will check it and correct it. Owners cannot edit or remove a review, its scores or its verdict.

We publish the bad with the good

Negative findings, like a narrow freight elevator or a noisy street, are published alongside the positives, in the same review.

Dates on prices and permits

Every price and permit rule shows the date we last checked it. Anything older than six months is flagged so you know to confirm it before you book.
