How to get there

Transit
Free shuttle from Broadview station every 30 minutes on weekends.
Driving
Paid parking on site; the lot is full by 10 am on Saturdays.
Walking
The Beltline Trail connects from Moore Park in 25 minutes.

Best light

By time of day

  • MorningMedium

    Mist over the ponds until about 8 am in autumn.

  • MiddayLow

    Little shade around the quarry.

  • AfternoonMedium

    Valley walls give soft side light.

  • Golden hourStrong

    Sun sets over the quarry ridge.

  • Blue hourLow

    Dark quickly in the valley.

By season

  • Spring

    Wildflowers in May.

  • Summer

    Saturday market crowds until 1 pm.

  • Autumn

    Peak colour in mid-October.

  • Winter

    Skating rink in the pavilion.
