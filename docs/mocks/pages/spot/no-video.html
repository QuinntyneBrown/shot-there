Trinity Bellwoods Park

  • Trinity Bellwoods
  • Park

Location and map

43.6476, −79.4137 · north of Queen St W between Strachan Ave and Shaw St

How to get there

Transit
501 Queen streetcar to Strachan Ave or Shaw St; the park is at the stop
Driving
Street parking on Shaw St and Gore Vale Ave, paid; fills on weekends
Walking
10 minutes from Ossington Ave at Queen St W

Light at Trinity Bellwoods Park by time of day
TimeRatingNote
MorningMediumDappled sun through the trees on the east side.
MiddayLowHard light on the lawns; patchy shade under the maples.
AfternoonMediumThe west path falls into even open shade.
Golden hourStrongLong light across the central lawn toward the gates.
Blue hourNonePath lights only near the gates; the lawns go dark.

By season

Conditions at Trinity Bellwoods Park by season
SeasonNote
SpringBlossom on the north side in early May.
SummerLawns are full on sunny weekends; go before 10 am.
AutumnThe maples turn in mid-October.
WinterSnow on the lawns; the bowl fills with sledders.
