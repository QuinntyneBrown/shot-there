How to get there

Transit
Take the 501 Queen or 508 Lake Shore streetcar to Park Lawn Rd, then walk 10 minutes south through Humber Bay Park East.
Driving
Park in the free lot at the foot of Park Lawn Rd. It fills by 10 am on summer weekends.
Walking
The bridge is on the Martin Goodman Trail, 25 minutes west of the Palace Pier along the water.

Best light

By time of day

  • MorningMedium

    Sun rises behind the skyline, so the arch is backlit until about 9 am.

  • MiddayLow

    Flat overhead light, harsh on the white arch.

  • AfternoonMedium

    Side light on the arch from the south-west.

  • Golden hourStrong

    West-facing, sun drops behind the skyline.

  • Blue hourMedium

    The downtown lights come on across the bay; bring a tripod.

By season

  • Spring

    Willows leaf out by mid-May. Paths are muddy after rain.

  • Summer

    Busiest season. Golden hour starts after 8:30 pm in June and July.

  • Autumn

    Best colour along the creek in the second half of October.

  • Winter

    Closed path after snowfall; the bridge itself is cleared.
